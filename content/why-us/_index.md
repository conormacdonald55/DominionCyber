## Most Adelaide families are more exposed than they realise.

You don't need to be a target to become a victim. Scammers don't discriminate — they look for unlocked doors. And most homes have several.

---

### Your home Wi-Fi is probably misconfigured.

Most routers ship with settings designed for convenience, not security. Default passwords, open ports, and outdated firmware are the first things an attacker looks for — and they're present in the majority of homes we visit.

The good news: these issues are fixable in an afternoon. The problem is most families don't know they exist.

---

### Your kids' devices are the weakest link.

School-issued laptops, gaming accounts, and social apps connect to your home network — and most have never been secured. Children's accounts are frequently targeted because they're less likely to be monitored and more likely to click on something they shouldn't.

---

### One phishing email can cost thousands.

Scams targeting Australian families have increased sharply. A convincing fake — from your bank, the ATO, or even "a friend" — is all it takes. The emails look legitimate. The pressure feels real. Most people don't realise until the money is gone.

---

### You can't protect what you can't see.

Most people don't know what's on their network, who can access it, or whether their data is being backed up. We find out so you don't have to guess.

In almost every home we assess, we find at least one device connected to the network that the homeowner didn't know was there — old phones, smart TVs, forgotten tablets. Each one is a potential access point.
