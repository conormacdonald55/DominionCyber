## About Dominion Cyber

Dominion Cyber was founded by Conor MacDonald, an Adelaide-based engineer with a background in technical systems and cybersecurity.

After years watching families and small businesses fall victim to avoidable digital risks — misconfigured routers, weak passwords, unprotected devices — Conor built Dominion Cyber around a simple idea: quality cybersecurity advice shouldn't require a corporate contract or an IT department.

Dominion Cyber works exclusively with Adelaide families and small businesses. Every engagement is handled personally. Every recommendation is practical, proportionate, and explained clearly.

Conor holds an ABN and operates with full professional indemnity insurance. His approach is methodical, discreet, and grounded in real-world experience — not vendor certifications.

> *"Most people just need someone to walk through the front door, look at what they actually have, and tell them the truth. That's what I do."*

## Why local matters

We're based in Adelaide. Not a call centre, not a franchise. When you book with Dominion Cyber, you deal with one person from first contact to follow-up. That's intentional.

There's no account manager between you and the person who actually does the work. If something needs following up after your visit, you email Conor directly — and he responds personally.

## How we work

**Discreet and professional.** We treat your home and your information with complete confidentiality. No data is collected beyond what's needed for the assessment. Our reports are yours — they go nowhere else.

**Plain English, always.** We don't impress you with technical language. We explain what's wrong, why it matters, and what we're doing about it. If something isn't clear, ask — we'll explain it differently.

**Genuinely experienced.** Dominion Cyber is built on a background in engineering and cybersecurity, not sales. We've seen the real-world consequences of poor digital hygiene — and we know what actually makes a difference.
