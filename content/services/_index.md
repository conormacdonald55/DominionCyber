## What We Do

Dominion Cyber provides professional, in-home cybersecurity consulting for Adelaide families and small businesses. We come to you, assess what you actually have, and fix what needs fixing — in plain English, without the corporate runaround.

---

### Home Network Security Audit

Your home router is the front door to your digital life. Most routers ship with settings designed for convenience, not security — default passwords, open ports, and outdated firmware are the first things an attacker looks for.

We review your router configuration, test your network for vulnerabilities, and harden your setup against common attacks. You'll know exactly what was wrong and what we did to fix it.

---

### Device Security Review

Every device on your network is a potential entry point. School laptops, smartphones, smart TVs, gaming consoles — each one matters.

We audit your devices for outdated software, weak security settings, and risky configurations. For families with children, we also configure parental controls and review app permissions.

---

### Account & Password Security

Weak or reused passwords are the most common cause of account compromise. We conduct a structured password audit across your key accounts — banking, email, social media — and help you implement a password manager properly.

---

### Phishing & Scam Protection

Australian families are increasingly targeted by sophisticated phishing campaigns. We review your email security settings, identify the most common attack vectors targeting your setup, and show you how to spot the fakes before they cost you.

---

### Secure Backup Setup

Most households have no reliable backup. We set up a practical cloud and local backup system so your family photos, documents, and important files are protected — even if a device is lost, stolen, or compromised.

---

### Smart Home & IoT Security

Security cameras, smart doorbells, voice assistants, and connected appliances all introduce risk if misconfigured. We audit your IoT devices, isolate them from your main network where appropriate, and ensure they're not broadcasting access to your home.

---

### Dark Web Monitoring

Your email address and passwords may already be circulating on the dark web from past data breaches. We set up monitoring so you're notified immediately if your credentials appear in future leaks.

---

### Plain-English Reporting

Every engagement ends with a written report in plain language — what we found, what we fixed, what you should watch for. No technical jargon. No confusing dashboards. Just a clear picture of where you stand.
