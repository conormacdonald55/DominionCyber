## Most Adelaide families are more exposed than they realise.

You don't need to be a target to become a victim. Scammers don't discriminate — they look for unlocked doors. And most homes have several.

**Your home Wi-Fi is probably misconfigured.**
Most routers ship with settings designed for convenience, not security. Default passwords, open ports, and outdated firmware are the first things an attacker looks for.

**Your kids' devices are the weakest link.**
School-issued laptops, gaming accounts, and social apps connect to your home network — and most have never been secured.

**One phishing email can cost thousands.**
Scams targeting Australian families have increased sharply. A convincing fake — from your bank, the ATO, or even "a friend" — is all it takes.

**You can't protect what you can't see.**
Most people don't know what's on their network, who can access it, or whether their data is being backed up. We find out so you don't have to guess.
